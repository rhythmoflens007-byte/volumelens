Volume-Normalized Market Representation for Quantitative Detection of Price–Flow Anomalies

How the charts are built, how the urgency signal was tested, and what it does not claim. Team 53, Hindustan Institute of Technology & Science.

MethodResultsLimitsReproduce
01

Method

Data. NSE cash-market 1-minute candles from AngelOne SmartAPI for 11 large caps (AXISBANK, HDFCBANK, ICICIBANK, INFY, ITC, PNB, RELIANCE, SBIN, TCS, TECHM, YESBANK), 85 sessions from June to September 2026, session window 09:30–15:29. A quality gate removes feed glitches (for example the day's running total reported as a minute's volume) before anything is built; every removal is published.

Volume Bricks. Box size N is the stock's average daily volume over its previous 20 sessions divided by 75 (Small 150, Large 40). A brick closes at the end of the minute in which N shares have traded, and runs from the previous brick's close to its own close.

N = mean daily volume (previous 20 sessions) / 75 normal = mean( median fill time of the last 50 bricks, median fill time in the same 30-min slot on previous sessions ) pace = fill time of the last 3 bricks / normal Urgency = max(0, 1 − pace) × 100 urgent ≥ 60 · watch ≥ 30

Target. A "large move" is a price range over the next 30 full minutes above twice the normal 30-minute range. Decisions are taken at the end of the minute a brick completes in; the outcome window starts at the next minute.

Alert model. LightGBM with monotone constraints on five signals (5-minute volume vs normal, recent range, opening hour, fill pace, VPIN), percentile-calibrated, with TreeSHAP reasons for every alert.

02

Results

Walk-forward: trained on earlier sessions only, one-session gap, tested on 74 unseen sessions (37,292 bricks, 3,996 large moves, base rate 10.7%). 95% intervals from 1,000 block-bootstrap resamples of test sessions.

03

What we don't claim

  • Direction. After 647 urgent zones price continued the same way 46% of the time, inside the chance range (46–54%). Urgency says a move is likely, not which way.
  • Who is trading. Urgency is not proof of institutions, algorithms or news.
  • Beating a good time-chart model. The alert score ties it; the value of the volume clock is seeing urgency on the chart, early and explained.
  • Sample. 11 large caps over about four months, 1-minute data (fill times are whole minutes). Mid-caps, other years and tick data are untested.
  • Not investment advice. An educational and research tool.
04

Reproduce it

Everything above is produced by the code; tests guarantee no look-ahead and no repainting, and the live server produces exactly the batch output.

python run.py # build bricks, indicators, alerts python backtest.py # walk-forward validation, bootstrap intervals python -m pytest -q # 16 engine and live tests npx playwright test # browser checks in Edge and Chrome
