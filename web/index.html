        How to read VolumeLens

        The idea
        A 5-minute chart gives every 5 minutes the same space, whether 10 people traded or 10,000. VolumeLens draws a new candle every time a fixed number of shares trade, so a burst of urgent volume becomes many candles instead of one.
        Urgent (orange)
        The last 3 volume candles filled in under 40% of their normal time. In testing, a big move (30-minute range over 2× normal) followed urgent bars about 2.3× as often as other bars. Urgency says something is happening, not which way the price will go.
        Time chart
        Normal 5-minute candles. Orange shading marks urgent minutes. Hover a volume candle to see exactly which minutes it covered.
        Fill time + VPIN
        Bars show how long each volume candle took to fill (short = urgent). White: smoothed fill time. Dashed: normal fill time for that moment. Blue: VPIN (0–1), how one-sided buying versus selling was over the last 50 candles.
        Absorption
        1 − actual price move ÷ expected move. Near 1: heavy volume but the price held. Shown for context only.
        Replay
        Plays the session candle by candle at market pace, so urgent bursts arrive fast. Every value you see was computable at that moment: nothing is redrawn later (checked by automated tests).
        Scanner
        Urgency score = share of the day's volume that traded in urgent bars. Burst = the largest run of urgent bars.
