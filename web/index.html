      How to read VolumeLens

      Time chart
      Normal 5-minute candles. Orange shading marks the minutes where volume flowed in unusually fast.
      Volume candles
      Each candle is a fixed number of shares, not a fixed number of minutes. The size adapts per stock from its previous 20 days. Orange candles filled fast: the last 3 bars took under 40% of their normal time.
      Fill time + VPIN
      Bars show how many minutes each volume candle took to fill; a short bar means volume arrived fast. The white line is the smoothed fill time, the dashed line is the normal fill time for that moment. The blue line is VPIN (0–1): how one-sided buying versus selling was over the last 50 candles.
      Absorption
      1 − actual price move ÷ expected move. Near 1: heavy trading but the price barely moved, so someone absorbed the flow. Below 0: price moved more than usual.
      Alerts
      A LightGBM model scores every candle for the chance of a big move (a 30-minute range over 2× normal). The top 4% of scores become alerts (at most 6 per stock per day), each listing the signals that drove it (TreeSHAP). Scores come from models that never saw that day.
      Controls
      Scroll to zoom, drag to pan, double-click a scale to reset. ← → step through volume candles. Esc fits the whole day.
