01The problem

A time chart gives a quiet minute and a frantic minute the same space.

When large orders arrive in a rush, thousands of trades land inside one candle. The chart looks calm while the market is not.

Time chart

One candle every five minutes

Ten trades or ten thousand, it prints one candle. Urgency stays invisible until the price has already moved.

VolumeLens

One brick every N shares

Busy stretches print many bricks, quiet ones print few. When bricks start filling fast, it shows as it happens.

02Method

Three definitions, and nothing else.

Box size

N shares, per stock

The stock's average daily volume over its previous 20 sessions, divided by 75. RELIANCE today: 1,20,000 shares.

Volume Brick

Renko on a volume clock

A brick prints every time N shares trade, and runs from the previous brick's close to its own close.

Urgency Index

How fast bricks fill

The last three bricks' fill time against normal for that stock and time of day. At 60 they filled 2.5× faster than normal.
