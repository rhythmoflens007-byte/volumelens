The problem

A five-minute candle can hide a thousand urgent trades.

When large orders arrive in a rush, a time chart still prints one candle. It looks calm while the market is not.

Time chart

One candle every five minutes

Ten trades or ten thousand, it prints one candle. Urgency stays invisible until the price has already moved.

VolumeLens

One brick every N shares

Busy stretches print many bricks, quiet ones print few. When bricks start filling fast, you see it as it happens.

How it works

Four ideas. Nothing hidden.

Every mark on the chart comes from these definitions, computed only from what was known at that moment.

01 · Box size
1,20,000shares · RELIANCE

Sized for each stock

The stock's average daily volume over its previous 20 sessions, divided by 75. Quiet stocks get small boxes, busy ones large.

02 · Volume brick
1 brick = N shares traded

Price on a volume clock

A brick prints every time N shares trade and runs from the previous brick's close to its own. Time stretches when trading rushes.

03 · Urgency Index
60+= urgent

How fast bricks fill

The last three bricks' fill time against normal for that stock and time of day. At 60 they filled 2.5× faster than usual.

04 · Flow
AccumulationDistribution

Volume that price absorbs

Net buyer-minus-seller volume over the last ten bricks. When it leans one way but price barely moves, someone is absorbing it.
