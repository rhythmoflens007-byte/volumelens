The problem

A calm candle can hide a storm.

When orders rush in, a time chart still draws one candle every five minutes. It looks calm. It isn't.

Time chart

Same candle, busy or not.

Ten trades or ten thousand, you get one candle. By the time it looks different, the move has happened.

VolumeLens

More bricks when it matters.

Busy minutes print many bricks. Quiet ones print few. A rush stands out the moment it starts.

How it works

Four simple ideas.

No black box. Every mark on the chart comes from these, using only what was known at that moment.

01 · Brick size
1,20,000shares · RELIANCE

Fair to every stock.

Each stock gets its own brick size, based on how much it usually trades.

02 · Volume clock
1 brick = N shares

A clock made of shares.

A new brick every N shares. Busy minutes stretch out. Quiet ones shrink.

03 · Urgency
60+= urgent

Urgency, as one number.

From 0 to 100. Above 60, bricks are filling 2.5× faster than normal.

04 · Flow
AccumulationDistribution

See volume being absorbed.

When buying or selling piles up but price won't move, VolumeLens marks it. Green for accumulation. Red for distribution.
