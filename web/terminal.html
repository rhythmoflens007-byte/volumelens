        Reading VolumeLens

        Volume Bricks
        A brick prints every time N shares trade, not after a fixed time. It runs from the previous brick's close to its own close, like Renko with volume in place of price. N adapts per stock from its previous 20 sessions (Small ≈ 150, Medium ≈ 75, Large ≈ 40 bricks a day). Busy stretches print many bricks; quiet ones print few. Urgent bricks glow.
        Urgency Index (0–100)
        How much faster than normal the last 3 candles filled. 60 and above is urgent (amber): under 40% of the normal time. 30–60 is watch. In testing, a large move (30-minute range over 2× normal) followed urgent candles several times as often as normal ones. Urgency means something is happening; it does not say which way the price will go.
